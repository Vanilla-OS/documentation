Almost was the on-demand immutability tool used during early Vanilla OS
development. It was replaced by ABRoot and is not part of Vanilla OS 3 Reunion.

Do not use Almost commands on a current installation. See the
[ABRoot documentation](abroot) for system upgrades, host packages, image
rebases, and rollback.
